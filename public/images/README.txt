Put your images here.

REQUIRED
  me.webp        Your photo. Square crop, around 300x300.
                 Used by the Profile widget and the About window.

RECOMMENDED
  og-cover.png   1200x630. This is the preview image people see when your
                 link is shared on WhatsApp, LinkedIn or Slack. Without it
                 the link previews as a blank box.

OPTIONAL
  projects/      Project thumbnails, referenced in src/data/projects.ts:
                   code-explainer.webp
                   fake-news.webp
                   sentiment.webp
                   mental-health.webp
                   kpi-dashboard.webp
                   reviews.webp
                 Missing files are fine — the cards fall back to a coloured
                 gradient, which looks deliberate rather than broken.
